# PaperFly Portfolio (41)

Build a complete, single-page, investor-grade Venture Portfolio website for "PaperFly", a circular economy venture designed for the COMQUEST Shark Tank Pitching Wars (E-Cell, Lady Shri Ram College for Women, University of Delhi).



---



### VISUAL DESIGN & THEMING SYSTEM (Match PPT Style Exactly)

- **Aesthetic**: Tactile editorial minimalism, eco-industrial, warm sustainable aesthetic. Clean modern typography, soft rounded corners, zero flashy gradient SaaS cliches.

- **Color Palette**:

  - Background Canvas: Natural warm unbleached paper cream (`#F7F4EE` or `#F5EFEB`)

  - Primary Headers & Accents: Deep Forest Ink (`#153327` / `#1B382B`)

  - Warm Highlight / Accent: Terracotta Kraft (`#C26D38`)

  - Card Containers: Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warm border (`#E5DFD5`)

  - Text: Dark Charcoal (`#222222`) and Slate Grey (`#555555`)

- **Typography**: Clean modern Sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`) for data, UI, and body text; elegant Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display` or `Newsreader`) for quotes, batch identifiers, and "The Note" showcase.

#### 3. Executive Summary & Problem Teardown

- 3 Clean Grid Cards:

  1. *The Delhi University Catalyst*: In 2024, the Delhi High Court halted DUSU vote counting over election paper defacement. Over 1.4L+ voters see streets buried in flyers printed at 30–40 paise/copy despite Lyngdoh Committee bans.

  2. *The Institutional Dilemma*: Colleges sit on tonnes of confidential exam scripts and admin records with zero certified destruction and no verifiable documentation for NAAC Criterion 7 audits.

  3. *The Broken Market*: Generic recycled paper is weak and bleeds ink, while premium eco-stationery (like WWF Eco A5) costs 3–4× mass-market prices (₹250–₹400+ vs ₹48 Classmate). PaperFly delivers quality at ₹65–₹99.



#### 4. Product Showcase & "The Note" Interactive Feature (Core IP)

- **Product Suite Cards**:

  - *Campus A5 Notebook*: 100 pages, 80 GSM smooth unbleached paper, kraft cover from recycled flyers. ₹79 MRP (₹59–₹69 with exchange).

  - *Corporate ESG Diaries (Phase 2)*: Executive planners with custom branding and verified BRSR waste disclosures. Avg ₹200.

  - *Government Registers (Phase 3)*: Heavy-duty registers via GeM file-weeding drives. Avg ₹125.

  - Add a toggle/callout explaining "Blind Sourcing": Batch-level tracking only (no student records or private data ever exposed; choice of Named, Group, or Anonymous institutional tagging).



#### 5. Operational Workflow (Interactive Tab Switcher)

- **Tab 1: The Micro-Sorting Protocol (At Source)**:

  - Visual 3-bin system:

    - `W (White)`: Exam sheets, admin files, notebook pages → hydro-pulped into 80 GSM writing pages.

    - `C (Colour)`: Uncoated flyers, posters → pressed into durable notebook covers.

    - `S (Secure)`: Locked tamper-evident bins for confidential answer scripts under a two-person rule.

- **Tab 2: Chemical-Free Partner Milling (Asset-Light)**:

  - 10-step mechanical flow: Sealed delivery → Mechanical hydro-pulping (zero caustic soda/bleach) → Multi-stage screening → Water-only washing → Gentle refining (reversing hornification) → Starch dry-strength additive → Cylinder-mould forming → Calendering & sizing → Batch QC (Cobb test, burst factor) → Binding.

  - Partnered with local western UP/Saharanpur job-work mills at ₹90/kg (Yr 1) to ₹64/kg (Yr 3).

- **Tab 3: Institutional Compliance & Destruction Guarantee**:

  - 48-Hour Destruction Certificate: Video logs and witnessed hydro-pulping.

  - PaperFly Green Campus Certification: Bronze (Bins + Impact Report) → Silver (90% sorting purity) → Gold (Zero-to-landfill + student return loop).



#### 6. Business Model & Unit Economics

- **Revenue Mix Breakdown (Year 3: ₹3.59 Cr Total)**:

  - B2B Circular Contracts (Colleges buy back at ₹65): ₹97.5L (27%)

  - Eco-Retail & Online Store (₹99 MRP / ₹449 5-pack): ₹82.0L (23%)

  - Corporate ESG Merchandise (Diaries at ₹200 avg): ₹80.0L (22%)

  - Government Supplies (GeM registers at ₹125 avg): ₹10.0L (3%)

- **Unit Economics Waterfall (Per A5 100-Page Notebook)**:

  - Raw waste collection & transport: ₹2.30 (Yr 1) → ₹1.80 (Yr 3)

  - Mechanical milling: ₹12.60 → ₹9.00

  - Cover board (Grade B flyer pulp): ₹3.20 → ₹2.90

  - Bound seed-paper Note insert: ₹4.50 → ₹3.50

  - Ruling & inner print: ₹1.50 → ₹1.20

  - Binding & finishing: ₹6.00 → ₹4.80

  - Packaging (plastic-free paper band): ₹1.00 → ₹0.80

  - Hub & mill logistics: ₹4.40 → ₹3.50

  - QC & wastage buffer: ₹1.50 → ₹1.50

  - **Total COGS**: **₹37.00** (Year 1) → **₹29.00** (Year 3 scale)

  - Blended Gross Margin: Scales from 50.3% to 58.9% by Year 3.
